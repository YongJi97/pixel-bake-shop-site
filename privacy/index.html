This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the iOS game Pixel Bake Shop: Idle Tycoon.

Summary

  • Your bakery save stays on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob can use advertising identifiers and related data if you allow tracking.
  • You can refuse tracking and still play; ads may still appear in a less personalized form when you choose to watch them.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Who we are

Pixel Bake Shop is developed and published by Yong Ji Chen. Contact: yongjichen97@gmail.com.

Information the app uses

Game save (stays on your device)

Progress, settings, and timers are stored as local data (JSON in the app’s UserDefaults / on-device storage). This save data is not uploaded by the game to our servers. We do not operate a game backend for this app.

Advertising (Google AdMob — only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad)

The app shows opt-in rewarded ads only. Ads do not play by themselves; there are no banners or forced interstitials. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), using AdMob App ID ca-app-pub-6858875783541494~5812138334.

Depending on your choices and region, Google may process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA / ADID) — only if you allow App Tracking Transparency (ATT) tracking when iOS asks.
  • Coarse location (for example, based on IP) — used by the ad network for ad eligibility and fraud prevention, not precise GPS from this game.
  • Device and ad performance / diagnostic data (for example crash and performance signals collected by Google’s mobile ads SDK).
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, whether a rewarded video was completed) so the reward can be granted and ads can be measured.

If you deny ATT, the game still works. Google may still serve ads without using the advertising identifier for tracking (non-personalized / limited ads, subject to Google’s and your region’s rules).

Consent in Europe (including the UK)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) may show a consent form before ads are requested. You can change or withdraw that consent through the controls Google provides (and through device settings described below).

What we do not collect ourselves

  • No player accounts, email capture, or social login inside the game.
  • No separate analytics SDK from us at this time (for example no TelemetryDeck or custom event backend).
  • No sale of personal information by us.

Why this information is used

  • Provide the game — keep your save and settings on the device.
  • Show optional rewarded ads — so the game can stay free to play.
  • Personalize ads — only when you allow tracking / give the relevant consent; otherwise ads are limited accordingly.
  • Measure and improve ad delivery — Google uses diagnostics and ad performance data as described in Google’s policies.

Third parties

Advertising and consent tooling are provided by Google:

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Related Google advertising and measurement services (as disclosed by Google)

Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy, AdMob & privacy, and how to control interest-based ads.

Apple may also process limited data as part of running iOS, App Tracking Transparency, and (if you use them) App Store features under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Do not watch ads — every core feature of the game works without watching a rewarded ad. Ads are optional bonuses.
  • App Tracking Transparency — on first ad setup, iOS may ask whether to allow tracking. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • AdMob / UMP consent — if you are in a region that shows a consent form (including the UK under our European regulations setup), you can accept or refuse the listed purposes; use Google’s privacy options / consent reset where offered.
  • Interest-based ads — opt out or manage ads via Google’s tools (for example adssettings.google.com) and Apple’s tracking / personalized ads settings.
  • Delete local data — deleting the app removes the on-device save. There is no cloud account for us to erase.

Children

Pixel Bake Shop is a general-audience idle game and is not directed at children under 13. It is not placed in Apple’s Kids category. Parents who believe a child has provided information through ads should contact us and Google as appropriate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own use.

International users

Ad serving and consent may depend on your region. For European regulations messaging we include the United Kingdom alongside the EEA and Switzerland. Data processed by Google may be handled in the United States or other countries as described in Google’s privacy policy.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s privacy practices change (for example if analytics or new ad formats are added). The effective date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
